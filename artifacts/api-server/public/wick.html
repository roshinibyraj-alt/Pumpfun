Recent closed trades · realized P&L

Paper-only BTC 5-minute market-order strategy. The signal version starts at 100 shares. When it earns $300 net realized profit in its active cycle, it pauses and the against-signal version starts at 200 shares. When the latter reaches its own $300 target, it resets to 100 and the signal version resumes at 100. Each version has separate capital, win rate, trade history, and equity curve. Entries remain taker market buys confirmed from next-tick visible asks; positions settle only from the original market’s official resolution, with no TP or SL. Paper history resets on process restart.
