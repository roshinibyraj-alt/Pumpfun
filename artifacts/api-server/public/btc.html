Every 5 seconds, independently place a 50-share UP and DOWN paper limit buy at that side’s midpoint minus $0.05. A limit fills only after a later fresh best ask reaches it. A fresh own-side bid at $0.99 or higher pays $1/share; a bid at $0.01 or lower pays $0/share and ends that window. All other positions in that window close at their own current bid (or $0 proceeds if no bid); pending entries are cancelled. Any positions still open when a new window begins also close at their own current bid (or $0 if no bid). No shares carry into the next window. Official market results are not used.
