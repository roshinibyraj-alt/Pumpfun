Tennis Paper Bot

Both players are eligible whenever they serve. The bot checks live score every second, buys 10 shares at server score 0–15 and another 10 at 0–30, then tries to sell that game's shares if the server wins the game. Shares from a lost serve game accumulate under that player and are sold only when the best bid is at least $0.10 above their weighted-average entry. No favorite, odds, game-score, or set filters.
