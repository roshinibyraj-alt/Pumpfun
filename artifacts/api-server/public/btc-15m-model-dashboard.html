Strategy rules

What the bot does and what this paper simulation does not claim.

Model decision15m candle ±1, 1h ±1, 4h ±2. A strong 15m extension-and-rejection can add ±2 mean-reversion points. Trade only at a total score of +2 or more for UP, or −2 or less for DOWN. Otherwise wait.
Limit ladderOne selected side receives six 100-share paper limits at $0.30, $0.25, $0.20, $0.15, $0.10, and $0.05. Maximum planned cost is $105. The other side receives no orders.
Fill and exitA fresh best ask must touch or cross down through a rung after placement; the paper fill is recorded at exactly that rung. Filled positions hold to resolution; no TP or stop-loss.
Paper orders do not fill immediately if the ask is already below a rung; they wait for a rise above and a later downward touch/cross. This simulation does not model queue priority, available maker liquidity, partial fills, fees, or rebates. Binance candle direction is a proxy; settlement uses the official Polymarket result.
