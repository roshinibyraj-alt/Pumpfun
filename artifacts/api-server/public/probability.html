Strategy rulesTaker entries · existing exits

Do nothing before 120 seconds. From then through the end of the window, monitor fresh UP and DOWN midpoints. When a midpoint first reaches $0.80 or higher, latch that side for the window and submit one 1,000-share limit buy at $0.40 and one 2,000-share limit buy at $0.60. The side is not changed and the trigger does not rearm.

Both limit orders remain resting through the window. Fresh visible asks at or below a limit can fill the order, at those ask prices; partial fills stay open and can accumulate. Unfilled shares are cancelled at window expiry. Open orders reserve their maximum limit cost plus a conservative fee estimate, but the reservation does not reduce displayed cash. Existing take-profit, stop-loss timing, and original-market resolution rules remain unchanged.

Take-profit is checked throughout the active window: a fresh own-side best bid ≥ $0.99 closes that lot and credits $1.00 per share before fees. Stop-loss remains gated until 295 seconds and uses a fresh own-side best bid ≤ $0.01 to credit $0.00 per share. Any lot still unsold at expiry waits for confirmed resolution of its original market; it is not closed at a later or last-seen CLOB price. Older unresolved shares stay out of the active-window accumulation boxes.

Paper only—no live orders. Each window can trigger once and place two resting limit buys on the first qualifying side. Existing TP/SL and market-resolution settlement rules remain in place.
