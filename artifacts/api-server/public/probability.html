Shrinking red (negative, rising) or solid green (positive, rising) sets UP. Shrinking green (positive, falling) or solid red (negative, falling) sets DOWN. Zero or unchanged histogram holds the bias. Direction persists until an opposite signal. Normal entries retain the net-edge gate and edge-reset cadence: staying above threshold does not trigger repeated buys. An opposite signal sells all old-side shares by sweeping actual bids, then targets the sold share quantity on the opposite side by sweeping asks.
