Strategy details

BTC FreqAI opening-score entries and shadow tiers

FreqAI trains a LightGBM classifier on Binance 1-minute OHLCV. Each completed minute is labeled with the 5-minute window containing its close, so the model score remains aligned with the active window as new one-minute candles close. Inputs use returns, candle shape, relative volume, volatility, and time within the window; Polymarket order-book pressure is not a model feature.

At each 5-minute window open, the bot captures the latest model score from the 1-minute candle that closed at that boundary. UP is selected when its score is at least 51%; otherwise DOWN must be at least 51%. If neither qualifies, or the opening score is not ready within five seconds, the window is skipped.

Three separate shadow ledgers latch fresh current-window scores strictly above 55%, 60%, or 65%, then wait for that side's ask strictly below $0.55, $0.60, or $0.65 respectively. Only an opposite qualifying score replaces a waiting signal; falling confidence alone does not cancel it. Signals expire at window end. Each tier buys at most once per window and sweeps only visible asks below its cap, with modeled taker fees.

The main strategy still makes at most one opening 50-share paper buy per window. Neither main nor shadow positions flip or use take-profit/stop-loss; open shares are held for official resolution. Freqtrade is prediction-only in dry mode; no live orders are sent.
