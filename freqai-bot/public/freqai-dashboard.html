Strategy details

BTC FreqAI · 58% only

FreqAI trains a LightGBM classifier on Binance 1-minute OHLCV. Each completed minute is labeled with the 5-minute window containing its close, so the model score remains aligned with the active window as new one-minute candles close. Inputs use returns, candle shape, relative volume, volatility, and time within the window; Polymarket order-book pressure is not a model feature.

After 45 seconds, a fresh current-window score strictly above 58% immediately requests the displayed size at available asks. The existing prediction model remains unchanged. No price offset or Binance fallback.

Base: 50 shares. After a loss, the next eligible window targets all outstanding recovery-cycle losses plus $10 net profit. Size is the smallest whole-share visible-ask sweep whose $1 winning payout covers purchase cost, market fees and that target. Reset to 50 when the cycle reaches +$10. No fixed doubling or three-step ladder. An unclear recovery result allows 50-share base trading; these wins and losses count toward the same cycle. Recovery waits if cash or visible depth cannot fund the full target. Large recovery sizes can exhaust paper capital; profit is not guaranteed.

TP: the held side’s fresh best bid at or above $0.99 immediately closes the paper position at a modeled $1 per share. SL: an explicit fresh $0.00 bid closes at zero. These paper outcomes update recovery immediately, but any recovery entry must wait until the next eligible window. Missing, crossed, empty or stale books never count as a zero bid. If neither threshold appears, wait for official resolution. Each position is paid and counted once.

One executed entry per window. Sweep fresh visible asks from cheapest upward and show the actual average entry. Insufficient depth results in a partial fill, with the unfilled remainder cancelled immediately. Nothing rests or rolls into the next market; opposite signals do not sell or add entries.

Taker fees are included in cost and P&L: shares × market fee rate × price × (1 − price), calculated for each fill. BTC 5-minute markets default to a 0.05 fee rate. No maker rebates are credited. Paper execution cannot guarantee live fills.

Paper TP/SL payouts release simulated capital and appear in realized paper P&L, clearly labeled separately from official resolution. The $1 TP is your requested simulation assumption, not a guaranteed executable sale price. No exit sale fees are charged because these are modeled terminal payouts, not market sales. No signal selling or live orders.
